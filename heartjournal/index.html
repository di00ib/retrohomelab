Help and Instructions

How to Take and Record Your Blood Pressure

1
Sit quietly for 5 minutes before taking your reading. Sit with your feet flat on the floor and your arm resting on a table at heart level.
2
Take your reading with your blood pressure monitor. Write down the two numbers — the top number (systolic) and the bottom number (diastolic).
3
Tap Add Reading at the top of this app and enter your numbers. Tap Save This Reading when done.
4
The app will show you right away whether your reading is normal, high, or low, and what it means.

Setting Up Supabase (Free Cloud Sync)

What is Supabase? It is a free, secure online storage service. Once set up, your readings automatically appear on all your devices — phone, tablet, computer — without you doing anything extra.
1
Go to supabase.com and tap Start your project. Sign up with your email address. It is completely free — no credit card needed.
2
Once logged in, tap New Project. Give it a name like “Heart Journal”. Choose a region close to where you live. Create a password (write it down somewhere safe). Click Create new project.
3
Wait about 1 minute for your project to be created. Then in the left sidebar, click the Settings gear icon, then click API.
4
You will see two things you need: the Project URL (looks like https://abcdefgh.supabase.co) and the anon public key (a very long string of letters starting with “eyJ”).
5
In the left sidebar, click Table Editor, then New Table. Name the table exactly: readings. Add a column named data (type: text) and a column named updated_at (type: text). Save the table.
6
Come back to this app, tap the Backup tab, paste your Project URL and your anon key into the boxes, and tap Save Settings. Then tap Test Connection — it should say “Connected!”
If you get stuck at any step, ask a family member, a friend, or the staff at your doctor’s office for help. You can also take a screenshot of what you see and show it to someone.

Creating a Report for Your Doctor

1
Tap the Reports tab at the top.
2
Tap Last 30 days from the Quick select menu, or choose your own date range.
3
Tap Download PDF Report. The file will download to your phone. You can then email it to your doctor, print it, or show it on your screen at your appointment.

When to Seek Emergency Care

Call 911 or go to the emergency room immediately if your top number is 180 or higher, OR your bottom number is 120 or higher. This is called a hypertensive crisis. Get emergency care right away, even if you feel okay.

Also contact your doctor soon if your readings are consistently above 140/90, or if you experience sudden severe headache, vision changes, chest pain, or difficulty breathing.

Common Questions

Is my information private?
Yes. This app never sends your information to us. Your readings stay on your device, and if you set up Supabase or Google Drive, they go to your own account that only you can access.
Can I use this on my phone and computer at the same time?
Yes, if you set up Supabase. Otherwise your readings are separate on each device. You can transfer them by downloading a backup on one device and loading it on another.
How do I install this as an app on my phone?
iPhone: Open in Safari → tap the Share button → tap “Add to Home Screen.”
Android: Open in Chrome → tap the three-dot menu → tap “Add to Home Screen.”
Is this app free?
Yes, completely free. No ads. No subscription. No hidden costs. Your data is yours.

Install as an App on Your Phone

iPhone: Open this page in Safari. Tap the Share button (the square with arrow pointing up at the bottom). Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. Tap Add. The app now appears on your home screen like a regular app.

Android: Open this page in Chrome. Tap the three dots in the top right corner. Tap Add to Home Screen or Install App. Tap Add or Install.

Windows computer: Open this page in Chrome or Edge. Look for a small install icon in the address bar. Click it and follow the prompts.
